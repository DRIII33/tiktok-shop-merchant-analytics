# Executive Summary: TikTok Shop Merchant Activation & Onboarding Optimization

## Business Context & Strategic Challenge
TikTok Shop's North American expansion depends on seller onboarding speed and immediate post-onboarding commercial velocity. An audit of 50,000 merchant records revealed that 46.8% of fully configured sellers failed to achieve a sale within their first 30 days, while catalog upload friction caused a 25% funnel drop-off.

## Key Analytical Findings
1. Catalog Setup Bottleneck: Product catalog registration represents the largest drop-off point in the seller journey, accounting for 25% of merchant drop-out.
2. A/B Portal Experiment Results: The streamlined onboarding flow (Variant v2.0) increased 30-day merchant activation from 48.0% to 58.0%, representing a statistically significant +10.0% absolute lift ($p < 0.001$).
3. Regional Disparities: The US_South region exhibited higher support ticket friction (1.45 tickets/merchant) compared to US_West (0.98 tickets/merchant).

## Strategic Recommendations
- Full Production Rollout: Immediately promote Onboarding Portal Variant v2.0 to 100% of incoming sellers.
- Self-Serve Catalog Tooling: Deploy automated product mapping tools to reduce catalog upload friction.
- Targeted Operational Support: Direct regional merchant assistance resources to high-friction categories (Apparel & Electronics).
